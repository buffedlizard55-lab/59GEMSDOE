What the competition actually targets

The task is geological fault presence — the organizer's words: "structures that are indicative of geothermal resources—namely, geological faults". It is not a geothermal-vent, temperature, flow or economic-reservoir label. Fault mapping supports exploration; on its own it does not establish a resource. Any claim in this repository about geothermal relevance is about why faults matter for exploration, never about a discovered resource.
