The measured finding worth carrying forward

The largest effect found in the H59-A session was a removal, not an addition. dist_visible_fault, visible_fault_dens_5k and visible_fault_dens_20k are computed from the catalogue pixels that form the training presence set. A model that sees them separates the training data (train loss −0.023; distinct predicted values collapse from 160,422 to 38,121) and then scores AUC 0.500 on withheld segments. Dropping all three lifts the holdout AUC from 0.500 to 0.8354.

Any future model in this repository should exclude those three channels unless it can demonstrate otherwise on a segment-level holdout. Receipt: evidence/leakage_channel_ablation.json.

Stated limitations

  • The dot budget is anchored to OWNER-REPORTED live evidence (~38–40k dots scored 0.2376–0.2778 across the group's artifacts); the spacing is anchored to this session's own holdout. The two are anchored differently and that is stated rather than hidden.
  • 2 holdout folds only. The t-interval with 1 degree of freedom is ±0.0121 and is not a meaningful confidence interval; the honest summary is the range.
  • The counterfactual mapping-effort correction failed (AUC 0.0122 — anti-correlated, because the clamped design is out-of-distribution for the gradient booster). Effort bias is only partly addressed. Reported as a negative result, not dropped.
  • The named confounder — drainage networks, which are linear, continuous at 1–10 km and concentrate exactly where the lidar scarp channels fire — cannot be separated from faults on this holdout, because the withheld positives are also catalogue faults.
