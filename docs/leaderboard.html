Why 0.2778 was the group's best — and what that implies

The published lineage is a sequence of emissions over one base field, not a sequence of better models: h25-1-dotted-h19-5-d1-5 0.2477 → d28-poisson300m-offcat 0.2600 → h27-4-solo-d28 0.2708 → h33-h33-2-b2 0.2778. The 0.2778 file is the 0.2708 dot set with every dot within 2 px of the published catalogue removed — a pure emission change worth +0.0070. That is consistent with the algebra above: the metric charges α = 0.2 per emitted pixel, so removing dots that cannot score is free money, and the catalogue-adjacent ones are exactly those.

Our reading: the marginal gains available in this family are in where the dots go and how many there are, not in a better classifier. That is what the break-even rule and the spacing sweep target.
