Method & evidence — intensity lane (H59-I)

Hypothesis and method status

The tested prototype asks whether a presence-only point-process intensity, zoned by expected new-fault pixels in 1 km blocks and then lattice-tiled above a holdout-derived break-even, can rank where the published fault catalogue is incomplete. The requested method is not complete: H59-I used uniform background and catalogue-derived trace-density/relief proxies. It did not implement an independent, same-effort target-group background per Phillips et al. (2009), because no defensible source was pinned. Do not call the historical result target-group corrected.

Historical mechanism. MaxEnt-form logistic model (Poisson point-process equivalence in form, Fithian & Hastie 2013) on 60,988 mapped presences and 120,000 uniform-background cells, using 13 geophysical/cat-proxy covariates. 1 km is the zoning block; expected new-fault intensity was summed over non-catalogue cells; the historical selected arm used a 4 px lattice and 2 px catalogue collar.

Named non-fault mimic. Mapmaking effort/survey footprint (access, map-sheet boundaries, flight-line levelling), plus lithologic contacts and basin hydrology that can generate lineaments without a fault.

Leakage canary and provenance caveats

The historical receipt labelled its per-feature test LEAKAGE-CANARY (AUC); the maximum reported AUC was 0.776 (e_dens5), below the 0.90 rule. This is a screen for individual features, not proof of external validity and not a fix for the historical case-control prior.

  • The current runner now calculates case-control prevalence from training-visible presences only. No rerun occurred after that correction.
  • The requested target-group background is NOT IMPLEMENTED; the response fault catalogue cannot be its own independent effort sample.
  • The historical full-catalogue geometry caches are not allowed as fold features. Holdout catalogue-derived features must be rebuilt from the visible mask.
  • The bridge raster hash is verified against a public GitHub mirror, not an independent organizer receipt.
