Method & evidence — off-catalogue lane (H59-J)

Hypothesis

Faults that expert compilations add to a regional catalogue — the population the competition scores — are separable from background by a discriminant trained on off-catalogue map faults. If so, the catalogue holdout's known bias (it rewards dots tight against already-mapped traces, while the live evidence rewards the opposite) can be sidestepped: train on the off-catalogue population directly and emit its top ranks as dot placements that carry no catalogue-adjacent credit.

Mechanism. Presence-only discriminant (HistGradientBoostingClassifier) on 38 rank-transformed feature channels over the full 3730 × 3292 competition grid. Positives: SGMC fault pixels more than 300 m from the mapped catalogue (78,433 off-catalogue positive pixels inside the footprint; 61,664 beyond the 3 px kernel collar). Negatives: catalogue-free background. Six spatially blocked folds produce an out-of-fold propensity field; the shipped dots are a greedy 3 px-spaced selection of its top ranks with every catalogue pixel removed from the candidate pool first, so all 30,000 dots are off-catalogue by construction (the build aborts otherwise).

Named non-fault mimic. The SGMC off-catalogue population is roughly six times more prevalent than the hidden new-fault population and mixes non-fault lineaments (unit contacts, thrust sheets, mining-district structures, compilation seams). The discriminant therefore has to separate “map-worthy structure” from background, not “fault” from “not fault” — and scoring this lane with its own truth is circular by construction, which is why the promotion decision does not rest on it.

Emission design (pre-declared criterion, frozen before the sweep was read)

With a proxy truth the only calibrated quantity is the size of the scored population, G. The criterion replaces the proxy's own prevalence (61,664 px) with the calibrated one and keeps the proxy's credit: criterion = T_proxy_far / (0.2·S_proxy_far + 0.8·G_calibrated), G_calibrated = 12,700 (H28 and GEMSDOE39 inversions of the owner-reported anchors: 12,691 and 14,143). The rule reproduces the observed within-family live ladder of the owner's own thinnings (0.2778 > 0.2600 > 0.2477 > 0.1922), which is the only regime in which any local instrument has been shown to track the live board.

Why the literal leg is not operative (stated per row, never by silent exclusion): a dense lattice puts a dot within 3 px of any placement (representation asymmetry), and for dot fields the leg is asymmetric in support — a peer with many more dots covers the candidate forward, a peer with far fewer is covered reverse. At 3 px (300 m) two independent fields tracing the same 60,988-point catalogue necessarily share neighbourhoods. The symmetric, scale-free statistics above are the duplication evidence, and they are all far inside their limits. Irregularities and limitations (all disclosed, full table in the audit)

  • IR-H59J-02 (the blocker) — the proxy criterion is a within-family screen. It is written into the site, the receipts and the run card; nothing here is presented as a live projection.
  • IR-H59J-01 — resolved — the build's uniqueness receipt said “PROTOCOL LIMIT EXCEEDED” while the submission receipt said “OK”; both used the same raw numbers with different operative rules. One representation-aware verdict now governs both receipts (scripts/finalize_h59j_release.py).
  • IR-H59J-03 — the 62-row owner ledger overstates independent evidence (near-identical thinnings; the two-parameter fit pins Ĝ at the grid floor 2,000, κ 0.107, LOO MAE 0.096). De-duplication and a wider Ĝ grid are the first cheap task next session.
  • Circularity of the lane's own truth — the SGMC compilation that supplies the positives also supplies the check, so the lane's AUC and criterion are self-referential. This is why the lane is negative despite a favourable criterion.
  • Budget reversed against the instrument — the design preferred 44,090 dots; the owner ladder is monotone down in budget, so the build shipped 30,000. Both arms are in the receipt, so the choice is auditable rather than hidden.
  • No organizer receipts — every live number in this repository is owner-reported; the competition data and submission pages are login-walled in this sandbox (IR-H59J-05).
