Method & evidence — intensity lane (current candidate H59-J)

This page records the lane's method. The current candidate is H59-J (structure-tensor ridge coherence consensus, 30,815 dots): its run card is docs/data/run_card.json, its method is hypothesis H1 in docs/research/hypotheses-v2.md, and its clearance numbers are in the panel below. The H59-I intensity lattice described in the next panel is an archived negative (holdout 0.08216 < the 0.135204 comparator) that is kept on the record, not promoted.

Hypothesis

A presence-only point-process intensity fitted to the mapped catalogue with mapping-effort covariates predicts where the faults the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue missed should sit. Zoning that intensity into 1 km blocks by expected new-fault pixels, and lattice-tiling only the blocks above break-even, places dots that beat a same-budget uniform-random control on the spatially-blocked hide-and-recover holdout.

Mechanism. MaxEnt-form logistic regression (equivalent in form to a Poisson point-process model — Fithian & Hastie, Annals of Applied Statistics 2013) on 60,988 catalogue presences vs 120,000 uniform background cells, 13 covariates: four geophysical edge planes (magnetic horizontal gradient, tilt derivative, gravity horizontal gradient, detrended-elevation slope), their element-wise max, five geophysical regime planes (TMI, conductivity, basement depth, geodetic second invariant, earthquake distance) and three mapping-effort planes (1 km and 5 km trace density, relief). Sample-selection bias is handled per Phillips et al. (Ecological Applications 19:181–197, 2009): effort covariates enter the model; a catalogue-density-weighted target-group background was rejected as circular (IR-59-EFFORT-01). Expected new-fault pixels per 1 km block = Σλ over non-catalogue cells (1 km deliberately exceeds the ~400 m catalogue registration error quoted in the brief). Break-even = ≥ 1 expected new-fault pixel per block (the ~1% lattice-payment floor).

Named non-fault mimic. Mapping-effort bias itself: the catalogue is a biased sample, so an intensity fitted to it inherits the mappers' footprint (access, quadrangle boundaries) rather than geology; magnetic flight-line levelling residuals and radiometric survey-block edges can also mimic lineaments.

Holdout protocol (shared tools, byte-identical to the published template)

Buffered whole-component leave-one-quadrant-out draw, seed 20 — the same draw and the same evaluator (gems57-pooled-hide-v2, implementation hashes match the published Session-5/7 receipts) as the 57GEMSDOE Session-5 arms, so the numbers are directly comparable. 11,321 withheld positive pixels; every catalogue-derived feature from the visible mask only; visible faults masked pixel-exactly; pooled DTI (α = 0.2, β = 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel); 1,000 paired percentile bootstrap draws over the physical 20 km spatial clusters.

Leakage canary (rule 4)

Each of the 13 covariates alone, discriminating withheld truth from the allowed domain, per fold: maximum AUC 0.776 (e_dens5), minimum 0.514 — no feature above the 0.90 bar, no flags. The two effort-density planes are catalogue-derived and therefore encode catalogue proximity (visible-only); that is disclosed, not hidden, and it is why they are the strongest single covariates.

Decile calibration (predicted vs withheld counts)

Pooled over the four folds, 68.5% of withheld-segment pixels fall in the top three deciles of predicted 1 km new-fault density (30% expected under uniformity): the zoning has real but modest skill. Full table: intensity_exp1_diagnostics_fixed.json.

SGMC diagnostic (independent state compilation, off-catalogue faults): Spearman(predicted density, SGMC-minus-catalogue block counts) = 0.068–0.153 by fold — weak positive, consistent with a zoning that points at some real off-catalogue faults. Per IR-57-INSTR-01 the SGMC proxy cannot rank placements and is used here only as this structural check.

Why the 3-px legs are not operative, stated per row rather than by silent exclusion. (1) IR-57-REPR-01: a continuous-surface peer has positive values over more than half the footprint, so every non-empty candidate has forward overlap 1.0 with it — the template's own shipped candidates fired this leg 43 times on a 213-raster certificate. (2) Support asymmetry: a peer with many more dots than the candidate covers the candidate's dots forward, and a peer with far fewer dots is covered reverse — both directions fire on independent fault-tracing fields. At 3 px (300 m) two dot fields that both trace the same 60,988-point catalogue must share neighbourhoods. The template's shipped candidates fired the same legs 95 and 85 times on full 695-raster scans and were still shipped. The symmetric, scale-free statistics above are the duplication evidence, and they are all far inside their limits.

Irregularities and limitations (all disclosed)

  • IR-59-EFFORT-01 — target-group background rejected as circular (the catalogue is the effort record); effort covariates used instead.
  • IR-59-EFFORT-02 — the GeoDAWN acquisition-block covariate is not used: the official block polygons are on USGS ScienceBase (DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ), which this sandbox's egress allow-list cannot reach, and the derived k-means proxy on windowed magnetic statistics was measured to be spatially non-contiguous (one 5.16 M-cell component). The lane's “distance to roads” effort covariate is likewise unobtainable here (USGS National Map / Census TIGER are off-allow-list); trace density and relief are the substitutes.
  • IR-59-DIAG-01 — the first diagnostic pass sum-pooled the per-cell expansion of block totals, inflating reported block densities 100×; rankings and all DTI values were unaffected. Fixed and re-derived in intensity_exp1_diagnostics_fixed.json.
  • Dot profile vs live evidence — the candidate's dots sit at median 5.0 px from the catalogue (min 2.24, p90 9.5), whereas the owner-reported top scorers sit near 19 px. The holdout rewards tight placement; the owner-reported board (n = 15) rewards distance. This disagreement is recorded, not tuned away.
  • Budget — the holdout-derived rule tiles 10,634 blocks and emits 71,119 dots (1.38% of the footprint), above the ~38–40k dots of the owner-reported top scorers; a budget-capped arm is the pre-registered next experiment (H59-I2), together with the block-level completeness-ratio zoning (new_b = Σλ·(1 − cat_frac/λ̄)) that should move dots further off the catalogue.
  • No scores claimed — no number here is a competition score; the organiser's public board and the owner-reported portfolio are context, not receipts.

Why the owner-reported 0.2778 is not explained here

The 0.2778 raster (GEMSDOE32 h33-h33-2-b2) is a 40,199-dot field minus 2,545 dots within 2 px of the mapped catalogue. Sparse thinning and catalogue-flank pruning reduce false-positive cost under the max-cover DTI, which is a plausible mechanism — but the scores are owner-reported, there is no organiser file receipt, and this session's holdout cannot confirm a causal gain. The 2 px collar finding here (+0.0074 DTI on the holdout) is consistent with that mechanism, nothing more.

Next session — pre-registered, none run

  • H59-I2 — gap zoning. Zone by c_b = cat_frac_b / λ̄_b (how much of the predicted intensity is already mapped) instead of by raw predicted new-fault density; per-block predicted new faults become n_b = Σλ·(1 − cat_frac_b/λ̄). Moves dots off already-mapped blocks.
  • H59-I2b — dot budget. The candidate emits 71,119 dots (1.38% of the footprint) against the ~38–40k of the owner-reported top scorers; tile the top-N blocks by n_b until the budget is spent.
  • H59-I3 — official effort covariates. USGS reliability-of-location class, distance to roads and the GeoDAWN acquisition block (ScienceBase DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ) are unobtainable through this sandbox's egress allow-list; only trace density and relief were used.
  • H59-I4 — off-catalogue holdout. Withhold whole catalogue components plus a 2 km buffer and score against the SGMC/state compilation in the withheld area. This is the instrument that could resolve the holdout-rewards-tight-placement vs live-evidence-rewards-distance disagreement.
