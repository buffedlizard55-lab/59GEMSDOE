Method & Evidence — Intensity Lane (Session H59-I2)

1. Pre-Registered Hypotheses & Physical Signatures

In the Intensity Lane, we zone the study area into 1 km × 1 km blocks (exceeding the ~400 m catalogue positional discrepancy reported by Hermant et al. 2025) by expected new-fault density, fit a presence-only inhomogeneous Poisson / MaxEnt point-process intensity (Phillips et al., Ecological Applications 19:181–197, 2009; Fithian & Hastie, Annals of Applied Statistics 7:1917–1939, 2013), and tile only blocks clearing break-even (~1–2% block density) using spacings from a holdout-derived utility table.

Following the Session H59-I post-mortem (REMAINING_WORK.md), we pre-registered four ranked hypotheses for Session H59-I2:

2. Mathematical Formulation & Resolved Irregularities

  • IR-59-EFFORT-02 (USGS/INGENIOUS QFaults Effort Join Resolved): Verified that record_id in data/external/trace_segments_utm11.csv (84,331 segments across 1,126 records) is the exact 0-based row index of data/external/qfault_attributes.csv (matching 60,988 / 60,988 = 100.0% of mapped pixels in existing_faults.tif). Extracted nearest-host FCODE2023 location-accuracy code (1=accurate: 714, 2=approximate: 331, 3=inferred/concealed: 81) and MAPSCALE (1:250,000: 846, 1:100,000: 280) strictly from visible faults.
  • IR-59-LOCO-01 (LOCO Two-Host Structural Frame): Using two_host_frame(visible) and a cKDTree over visible pixels, every pixel on a visible fault component C1(x) is assigned its nearest other visible component d1(x) ← d(x, C2(x)) (10th / 50th / 90th percentiles = 3.16 / 9.00 / 29.97 px) and second-nearest other component d2(x) ← d(x, C3(x)) (10th / 50th / 90th percentiles = 7.00 / 16.64 / 45.28 px), matching the inter-component distance distribution of withheld faults (4.00 / 9.06 / 25.55 px) with zero access to withheld truth.
  • IR-59-LOCO-EFFORT (Self-Component Effort Subtraction): Raw 5 km and 10 km fault-density kernels evaluated on a long fault trace include the trace's own pixels, creating artificial training discrimination (AUC ≈ 0.903). Subtracting min(|C1(x)|, L_window) / L_window^2 removes the self-component contribution so e_dens_5km_loco and e_dens_10km_loco measure strictly surrounding mapping effort (training LOCO AUC drops to 0.6408 and 0.6009; max AUC across all 31 covariates is 0.7715 < 0.90 on holdout and 0.7439 < 0.90 on training LOCO).
  • IR-59-LATTICE-MOD10 (Modulo-10 Block Lattice Wraparound Fix): Session H59-I's tile_blocks computed (ay + np.arange(0, 10, s)) % 10, which wraps 9 ≡ -1 (mod 10) for s = 3, placing dots 1 px apart inside every block and across block boundaries. Fixed in tile_blocks (non-wrapping offsets) and tile_blocks_corridor (cross-block greedy 300 m triangular-kernel expected-reward maximization E[w_300m · λ]).

5. Descriptive Holdout Orientation & Distance Structure

Evidence class: HOLDOUT-STRUCTURE (descriptive, not a score). From evidence/orientation_structure.json, withheld fault pixels have nearest-visible-catalogue distance 10th / 50th / 90th percentiles of 4.00 / 9.06 / 25.55 px (sample unit: fault raster pixel; not segment-weighted; minimum local coherence 0.2; unsigned axial difference in degrees, folded to [0, 90]). Withheld angle pixels (count; % of valid sample) concentrate in sub-parallel and low-angle Riedel orientations (0–15° and 15–30°), which is captured in H59-I2 by the strike-aligned DEM scarp coherence covariate g_scarp_rel and the two-host strike alignment s_cos2_s12.
