## Named Non-Fault Mimic
- **Mapping-effort & compilation boundary artifacts:** 1:24,000 / 1:100,000 quadrangle boundaries and road-corridor access patterns where catalogue trace density steps up sharply without a change in tectonic strain rate.
- **Topographic & geophysical non-fault lineaments:** Non-tectonic fluvial terrace risers, shoreline scarps of Pleistocene Lake Lahontan, and erosional range-front breaks in 3DEP detrended elevation (`Band 16`), as well as aeromagnetic flight-line levelling residuals (`Bands 1–2`) and pluton-margin gravity gradients (`Band 10`).
